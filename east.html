✦ The Soulful East ✦

Tigers, Tea
& Tagore

East India is the region of poets and rebels, of wild jungles and monsoon rains. Kolkata — the City of Joy — was India's intellectual capital for two centuries. Darjeeling's tea is sipped by kings. The Sunderbans hides the last wild tigers.

The east is also home to the world's living root bridges in Meghalaya — entirely made of living fig tree roots, hundreds of years old and still growing. Odisha's Konark Sun Temple is a UNESCO masterpiece of stone astronomy.

Icons of the East

01

Darjeeling · West Bengal · Queen of the Hills · 2042m

Darjeeling

The "Queen of Hills" sits at 2,042m in West Bengal, cradled between the Himalayan peaks. The terraced emerald tea gardens here produce the world's most prized tea — Darjeeling First Flush is called the "Champagne of teas." The UNESCO-listed Darjeeling Himalayan Railway — the beloved "Toy Train" — has steamed through these hills since 1881, taking 7 hours to climb from Siliguri to Darjeeling through thick forests and waterfalls. On clear mornings, Kanchenjunga — the world's third-highest peak — rises above the clouds like a pink-gold wall. The Tiger Hill sunrise, where the Himalayas blush before dawn, is one of India's most treasured sights.

02

Kolkata · West Bengal · City of Joy · Former British Capital

Kolkata

Kolkata was the capital of British India until 1911 and remains India's most intellectually alive city. The Victoria Memorial — a gleaming white marble palace set in lush gardens — is one of the most beautiful buildings in Asia. The Howrah Bridge, built without a single nut or bolt in 1943, carries 100,000 vehicles every day. Kolkata gave India four Nobel Prize winners including Rabindranath Tagore, the first non-European Nobel laureate. Every October, Durga Puja transforms the entire city — 3,000 elaborately designed temporary shrines called pandals spring up across every neighbourhood. The whole city dances, sings, and feasts for 5 days straight.

03

Sunderbans · West Bengal · UNESCO World Heritage Site

Sunderbans

The Sunderbans is the world's largest mangrove delta — 10,000 sq km of tidal forest, river channels, and mudflats where the Ganga and Brahmaputra rivers meet the sea. It is home to the Royal Bengal Tiger — the only tigers in the world that swim in saltwater. Around 300 tigers live here, and they are famously elusive. The entire ecosystem is a UNESCO World Heritage Site. Boat safaris through the dense channels are eerie and extraordinary — sounds of birds and splashing water, no roads or roads at all, no electricity. Spotting a tiger here — even its paw print in the mud — creates memories for a lifetime.

🐅 Royal Bengal Tiger🌿 World's Largest Delta🚤 Boat Safari🦅 Rare Birds🏛️ UNESCO Heritage

✦ Culture of the East ✦

Poetry, Colour
& Wild Spirit

Festival · October · Kolkata

Durga Puja

The entire city becomes a gallery of art for 5 days. 3,000 temporary shrines, each more elaborate than the last, fill Kolkata with music, light, and 10 million people celebrating together.

Heritage · Darjeeling

First Flush Tea Harvest

March–April sees the "First Flush" — the year's first tea leaves plucked by hand. Sold at auction for up to $1,000 per kg, this is the rarest tea in the world. Visit an estate and pluck your own leaves.

Wildlife · Sunderbans

Royal Bengal Tiger Safari

The only tigers in the world that swim in salt water. Silent boat safaris through tidal channels — the forest breathes and watches back. One of the most intense wildlife experiences on Earth.

UNESCO Heritage · Odisha

Konark Sun Temple

Built in 1255 CE as a chariot of the Sun God with 12 pairs of giant stone wheels. The wheels are also precise sundials — still accurate to the minute. A UNESCO masterpiece of stone astronomy.

✦ Taste the East ✦

Flavours of Tagore's Bengal

East Indian food is delicate, aromatic, and built on mustard oil, panch phoron (five spices), and freshwater fish. Bengal's sweets — rosogolla, sandesh, mishti doi — are legendary across the world.

West Bengal

Rosogolla

Soft chenna (cottage cheese) balls soaked in sugar syrup — invented in Kolkata in 1868 by Nobin Das

Kolkata

Kolkata Biryani

With a boiled egg and potato inside — unique to Kolkata, a Nawabi legacy from Lucknow

Darjeeling

Darjeeling First Flush Tea

The "Champagne of teas" — light, floral, and aromatic. Sip it plain with no milk to taste the Himalayas

Darjeeling / Sikkim

Momos

Steamed dumplings with pork or vegetables — the comfort food of the Himalayan east
