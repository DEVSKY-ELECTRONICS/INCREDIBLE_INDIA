Best Entry Points

Delhi (North India), Mumbai (West), Kochi (South Kerala), Kolkata (East). All have major international airports. Delhi is usually the best starting point for first-time visitors.

📅

Best Time to Visit

October–March is perfect India season — cool, dry, clear. March–June is hot but good for the mountains. June–September is monsoon — lush but wet. Avoid May–June for the plains.

🛂

Visa Information

Most nationalities can apply for India e-Visa online at indianvisaonline.gov.in. Takes 48–72 hours. Cost is approximately ₹4,000–9,000 depending on your country. Valid for 30 or 60 days.

💰

Budget Guide

Budget: ₹1,500–2,500/day (hostels, street food) · Mid-range: ₹4,000–8,000/day (hotels, restaurants) · Luxury: ₹15,000+/day (heritage palaces, fine dining)

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before your India journey.

Is India safe to travel?+

India is generally safe for tourists. The main tourist areas — Delhi, Agra, Jaipur, Goa, Kerala, Mumbai — are all well-patrolled with good tourist infrastructure. Use standard precautions: keep copies of documents, use app-based taxis (Ola/Uber), drink bottled water, and buy travel insurance. Female travellers should research their specific destinations.

What vaccinations do I need?+

Consult your doctor 6–8 weeks before travel. Commonly recommended: Hepatitis A, Typhoid, and Tetanus. Malaria prophylaxis is recommended for rural jungle areas (Sunderbans, Odisha). Ensure your Routine vaccinations (MMR, Polio) are up to date.

What is the currency and how do payments work?+

Indian Rupee (INR). ATMs are widely available in all cities. UPI (digital payment via QR codes) is universal — even street food vendors accept it. Most hotels and restaurants in tourist areas accept international cards. Always carry some cash for small purchases and auto-rickshaws.

How do I get around India?+

Indian Railways is the world's 4th largest rail network — book at irctc.co.in (book 2–3 months ahead for popular routes). Domestic flights are cheap (₹1,500–4,000 one way). For local travel use Ola/Uber apps. For Rajasthan, hire a private car with driver for 1–2 weeks.

What should I wear in India?+

India is conservative outside beach resorts. Cover knees and shoulders when visiting temples, mosques, and religious sites. Lightweight cotton is ideal for the heat. A dupatta (light scarf) is useful for women. In Rajasthan, a light shawl for cold desert nights. In Ladakh and Darjeeling, pack warm layers.

What are the must-see places for a first-time visitor?+

The Golden Triangle (Delhi–Agra–Jaipur) in 7 days is a classic first visit. Add Varanasi for spiritual India (2 days). Then extend to Goa or Kerala for beaches and nature. For a 2-week trip: Delhi → Agra (Taj Mahal) → Varanasi → Jaipur → Goa = the perfect introduction to India.
