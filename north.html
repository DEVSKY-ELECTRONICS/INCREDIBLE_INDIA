✦ The Eternal North ✦

Mountains, Moghuls
& Mysticism

North India is where civilisation began on this subcontinent. The Ganga civilisation, the Mughal Empire, the Sikh Golden Temple, the Himalayan monasteries — all of history converges in one breathtaking region.

The Golden Triangle — Delhi, Agra, Jaipur — is the world's most visited circuit. But the real North runs deeper: the smoke of Varanasi's ghats, the stillness of Ladakh at 4,000m, Kashmir's saffron-scented air in autumn.

✦ Must-See Places ✦

Icons of the North

01

Agra · Uttar Pradesh · UNESCO World Heritage Site

Taj Mahal

Emperor Shah Jahan spent 22 years and 20,000 artisans building this ivory-white marble mausoleum for his beloved wife Mumtaz Mahal who died in 1631. The Taj shimmers pink at dawn, blinding white at noon, and silver-gold under a full moon. Its 300m reflection pool creates the world's most photographed mirror image. The interior features pietra dura — precious stone inlay with lapis lazuli from Afghanistan, jade from China, turquoise from Tibet. Declared a New Seven Wonder of the World in 2007. Visit at sunrise when the monument seems to float on morning mist.

🕌 Mughal Architecture🌅 Sunrise Visit🌕 Full Moon Night💎 Pietra Dura Inlay🏛️ UNESCO Heritage

02

Srinagar · Jammu & Kashmir · Heaven on Earth

Kashmir

Mughal Emperor Jahangir called Kashmir "paradise on earth" in the 17th century. Dal Lake at dawn — wooden Shikara boats drifting through mist, lotus flowers floating, snow-capped Pir Panjal reflected — is the most peaceful sight in Asia. Ride a Shikara at 5am as flower vendors row past with marigolds, then breakfast on a houseboat. Kashmir has four seasons of magic: spring tulips at Shalimar Bagh, summer meadows in Pahalgam, autumn chinar trees blazing red, and Gulmarg powder snow for world-class skiing.

03

Varanasi · Uttar Pradesh · The Eternal City · 3000+ Years Old

Varanasi

The oldest continuously inhabited city on Earth — over 3,000 years. Ganga Aarti at Dashashwamedh Ghat every evening is a choreographed ritual of fire, incense, conch shells, and Vedic chants by priests in perfect synchrony — it has happened every single evening for centuries. Take a wooden boat at 5am as priests light morning fires on the ghats, pilgrims wade into the Ganga, and the city opens its eyes to another eternal day — one of the most profoundly human experiences anywhere on Earth. Tulsidas wrote the Ramcharitmanas here. Sarnath (10km) is where Buddha gave his very first sermon.

04

Leh · Ladakh · 3500m Above Sea Level · Land of High Passes

Ladakh

At 3,500m above sea level, Ladakh is unlike anywhere on Earth. Pangong Tso Lake (134km long, shared with China) changes colour from brilliant sapphire to emerald to silver across the day — where "3 Idiots" was filmed. Thiksey Monastery looks exactly like Tibet's Potala Palace perched on a cliff. The Nubra Valley has golden sand dunes and double-humped Bactrian camels — desert and Himalayas in one shot. The Milky Way here blazes with zero light pollution. Manali–Leh Highway is the world's most spectacular road trip at 5,000m altitude passes.

💙 Pangong Tso Lake🏯 Thiksey Monastery🏍️ Manali–Leh Highway🌌 Milky Way Stargazing🐪 Nubra Valley Camels🎭 Hemis Festival July

05

Amritsar · Punjab · Sikh Holiest Shrine

Golden Temple

Harmandir Sahib — covered in 750kg of pure gold — sits in the centre of the sacred Amrit Sarovar (Pool of Nectar). The Langar (community kitchen) serves free vegetarian meals to 100,000 people every single day — the world's largest free kitchen, open to any person of any religion. Arrive before sunrise: the temple glows amber on the still water as kirtan (devotional music) flows from inside — among the most moving sights in the world. Also visit Wagah Border flag ceremony at sunset (30km away) for electrifying patriotism.

06

Rishikesh · Haridwar · Uttarakhand · Yoga Capital of the World

Rishikesh & Haridwar

Haridwar's Har Ki Pauri is where the Ganga leaves the Himalayas and enters the plains — Ganga Aarti here every evening with thousands of floating diyas creates a river of living fire. Rishikesh (25km) is the world yoga capital — where the Beatles came in 1968 to meditate with Maharishi Mahesh Yogi. Today: 200+ yoga ashrams, Grade IV white-water rafting, India's highest bungee jump, and the iconic Ram Jhula and Laxman Jhula suspension bridges. Both spiritual and thrilling at once.

✦ Cultural Highlights ✦

Moments That Move You

Festival · March · All North India

Holi — Festival of Colours

Streets explode in coloured powder — gulal, water guns, music. Best in Mathura, Vrindavan and Barsana where it lasts a full week.

Festival · Oct–Nov · All India

Diwali — Festival of Lights

A million clay oil lamps light every home. At Varanasi's ghats, floating diyas turn the Ganga into a river of living fire on Dev Deepawali.

Harvest · October · Pampore, Kashmir

Saffron Harvest, Pampore

Every October the Pampore plains turn purple with crocus flowers. Farmers hand-pluck red stigma before sunrise — ₹3–5 lakh per kg, the world's most precious spice.

Festival · June–July · Hemis, Ladakh

Hemis Festival, Ladakh

600-year-old masked Cham dance by Tibetan Buddhist monks. The monastery giant thangka — displayed once every 12 years — is beyond description.
